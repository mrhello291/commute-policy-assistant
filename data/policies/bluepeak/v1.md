# Bluepeak Logistics Commute Policy

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Who this covers

This policy covers employee transport for all Bluepeak Logistics staff at the Hyderabad and Chennai
offices and warehouses.

### 1.2 Key terms

- **Trip**: one pickup or one drop.
- **Night drop**: a drop in the night window in section 4.1.
- **No-show**: a booked trip the employee did not take, as defined in section 8.
- **Transport Head**: the manager in charge of transport at each site.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who is covered

Employees of every grade are eligible for company transport. Contract staff supplied by a staffing
agency are not covered; their agency arranges their travel.

### 2.2 Distance from office

Transport is provided when the employee's home is more than 3 km from their work location. Employees
living 3 km or closer may still use the shuttle from the nearest metro station.

### 2.3 Service area

Transport is provided up to 30 km from the work location by road. Beyond that, the employee makes
their own arrangements.

### 2.4 Registration

Employees register their home address once in the Bluepeak commute app and may update it at any time.
There is no minimum period of use.

## 3. Booking and cut-off times

### 3.1 Booking cut-off

Pickup and drop requests must be raised at least 2 hours before the scheduled trip time.

### 3.2 Cancellations

Trips can be cancelled up to 1 hour before the scheduled time without penalty.

### 3.3 Waiting at the pickup point

The cab waits 10 minutes at the pickup point after the scheduled time. After that it leaves and the
trip is recorded as a no-show.

### 3.4 Changes of address

A change of home address is updated in the commute app. A temporary change of address of up to 7 days
needs the approval of the site Transport Head.

## 4. Night-shift travel

### 4.1 Night travel window

Drops scheduled between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM are night drops.

### 4.2 Security escort

For every drop after 10:00 PM with a woman employee on board, a security escort accompanies the cab.
The route is planned so that a woman employee is not dropped last without the escort present.

### 4.3 Consent

A woman employee gives written consent before she is first rostered on night shifts. Without that
consent she is not rostered for night drops.

## 5. Weekend and holiday travel

Company transport is not provided on Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays. Employees asked to work
on those days may claim reimbursement of up to INR 500 per day for their own travel, with their
manager's approval.

## 6. Ad-hoc and airport trips

### 6.1 Ad-hoc trips

Ad-hoc trips must be approved by the site Transport Head.

### 6.2 Airport trips

Airport pickups and drops for official travel are approved by the site Transport Head. The cost cap
for an airport trip is INR 2,000.

## 7. Cost caps

Regular trips are capped at INR 1,000 per trip. A trip expected to cost more than the cap needs the
Transport Head's approval before it is booked.

## 8. No-show policy

A no-show is recorded when the employee does not board a booked cab and did not cancel at least
1 hour before the trip. The first no-show in a calendar month results in a reminder. For each further
no-show in the same month, INR 250 is deducted from the employee's salary. The employee is told in
writing before a deduction is made and has 5 working days to dispute it with HR. The count starts
again each month.

## 9. Guest travel

Guests may travel in a company cab with prior HR approval, provided the host employee travels with
them.

## 10. Safety and incidents

Every cab is GPS-tracked. Report any incident, breakdown or unsafe behaviour to the 24x7 helpline
straight away. Employees carry their company ID card in the cab.

## 11. Review of this policy

HR and the Transport Heads review this policy once a year. When a new version takes effect, the
earlier version stops applying.

## 12. Contact

Reach the Bluepeak transport desk at transport@bluepeak.example or on the 24x7 helpline 1800-000-222.
