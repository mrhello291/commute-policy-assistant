# Arcadia Software Employee Transport Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy explains who can use company transport at Arcadia Software, how to book it, and the
rules that apply to every trip. It applies to all Arcadia employees working from the Bengaluru and
Pune offices. Where this policy is silent, contact the transport desk.

### 1.2 Key terms

- **Rostered trip**: a regular pickup or drop registered in the transport app.
- **Night travel**: any trip covered by the night travel window in section 4.1.
- **No-show**: a booked trip the employee did not take, as defined in section 8.
- **Transport desk**: the Arcadia team that runs transport and answers questions about this policy.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Grades

Employees in grades L1 to L5 are eligible for company cabs. Employees in grade L6 and above receive a
monthly car allowance instead and are not eligible for rostered cabs.

### 2.2 Distance from office

Company transport is provided only when the employee's registered home address is more than 5 km
from the office by road. Employees living 5 km or closer are not eligible for regular pickups or
drops.

### 2.3 Contractors, interns and temporary needs

Contractors and interns are not eligible unless their manager and HR both approve in writing. An
employee with a medical or other temporary need may be approved by HR for a fixed period, and the
approval states an end date.

### 2.4 Service area

Company transport is not provided where the registered home address is more than 35 km from the
office by road, even if the employee meets sections 2.1 and 2.2.

### 2.5 Registration

An eligible employee registers for rostered transport in the transport app. Registration is for a
minimum of 3 months. A request to opt out made after the 10th of a month takes effect from the first
day of the next month.

## 3. Booking and cut-off times

### 3.1 Booking cut-off

Pickup and drop requests must be submitted in the transport app at least 4 hours before the
scheduled trip time. Requests received after the cut-off are not guaranteed.

### 3.2 Cancellations

A trip can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. A trip cancelled later than that,
or not cancelled at all, is recorded as a no-show.

### 3.3 Waiting at the pickup point

The cab reaches the pickup point up to 10 minutes before the scheduled time and waits 5 minutes
after it. If the employee has not boarded by then, the cab leaves and the trip is recorded as a
no-show. Employees can follow the cab's live location in the transport app.

### 3.4 Changes of address

A permanent change of home address must be reported in the transport app with address proof at least
2 working days before it is needed. A temporary change of address of up to 15 days needs the
reporting manager's approval and must stay inside the service area in section 2.4.

## 4. Night-shift travel

### 4.1 Night travel window

Any drop scheduled between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM is treated as night travel and follows the rules in
this section.

### 4.2 Security escort

A trained security escort travels in the cab on every drop after 8:00 PM that carries a woman
employee. A woman employee is never the first person picked up or the last person dropped on a night
route unless an escort is on board.

### 4.3 Safe-reach confirmation

After a night drop, the employee must confirm safe arrival in the transport app within 30 minutes.
If no confirmation is received, the transport desk calls the employee.

### 4.4 Consent and choice

A woman employee gives written consent before she is first rostered on night shifts, and can withdraw
it with notice to HR. She may decline any cab she does not feel safe in and call the transport desk
for another vehicle, without penalty.

### 4.5 When an escort is not available

A night drop that needs an escort is not released until an escort is assigned. If none can be
assigned, the transport desk arranges another escorted vehicle. The employee is not charged and no
no-show is recorded.

## 5. Weekend and holiday travel

Cabs on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are available only with prior approval from the
employee's L2 (skip-level) manager. The approved request must reach the transport desk by 5:00 PM on
the preceding Thursday. An approval covers only the dates named in it.

## 6. Ad-hoc and airport trips

### 6.1 Ad-hoc trips

An ad-hoc trip outside the regular roster needs approval from the employee's reporting manager in
the transport app before the trip is booked.

### 6.2 Airport trips

Airport trips for official travel are approved by the employee's reporting manager. The cost cap for
a single airport trip is INR 1,500. The transport desk does not arrange airport trips for personal travel.

## 7. Cost caps

The cost cap for a standard rostered trip is INR 800 per trip. Any trip expected to cost more than
the cap needs written approval from Finance before it is booked. A trip booked above the cap without
that approval is charged to the employee's cost centre and reported to the reporting manager.

## 8. No-show policy

A no-show is recorded when the employee does not board a booked cab and did not cancel in time.
Two no-shows in a calendar month lead to a written warning, which is copied to the reporting manager.
A third no-show in the same calendar month leads to suspension of transport for 7 days. The count
starts again on the first day of each month. A trip is not counted as a no-show if the cab arrives
more than 15 minutes late or the transport desk cancels it.

## 9. Guest travel

Company cabs may not be used to transport guests, family members, friends or visitors. Guests must
make their own travel arrangements.

## 10. Safety and incidents

### 10.1 Vehicles and drivers

Every cab is GPS-tracked and has a panic button. Drivers are police-verified. Employees carry their
company ID card in the cab and wear seat belts.

### 10.2 Reporting an incident

Report any incident, breakdown or unsafe behaviour to the transport desk straight away. The desk
is staffed at all hours. Serious incidents are escalated to the head of HR the same day.

## 11. Review of this policy

HR and the transport desk review this policy every six months, and after any safety incident.
When a new version takes effect, the earlier version stops applying.

## 12. Contact

Questions about this policy go to the Arcadia transport desk at extension 4400 or
transport.desk@arcadia.example.
